## Description
Wakelatch is an alarm for people who sleep through alarms, or switch them off without really waking up.

Set two times. Ideal is when you'd like to be up. Deadline is when you have to be.

HOW A MORNING GOES
• Two soft nudges, 20 and 10 minutes before Ideal.
• From Ideal, it rings every few minutes. Stop snoozes, or tap I'm up and solve a challenge.
• From the Deadline, it rings every minute and Stop no longer works. Only the challenge ends it.
• Five and twelve minutes later, a quick "still up?" check makes sure you didn't drift back to sleep.

CHALLENGES
Math, typing a sentence, keeping your eyes open (Face ID camera), walking, or shaking the phone. Pick one for before the deadline and one for at the deadline. Later rings ask for a little more.

RELIABLE
Wakelatch uses Apple's alarm system, so it rings through silent mode and Focus, on your Apple Watch too, and even if the app is closed or the phone restarts.

BEDSIDE MODE
Leave Wakelatch open on the charger at night and the alarm rings full screen in the app with its own sound.

FAIR
Two emergency skips a month, for the morning a challenge can't be done.

PRIVATE AND FREE
No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no network code. Nothing you do in Wakelatch leaves your phone. Open source under the MIT license.

Requires iOS 26.2 or later.

## Review notes
Wakelatch schedules AlarmKit alarms. To see a morning quickly: set Ideal two minutes from now and Deadline two minutes after that, then wait. With the app open and Bedside mode on, the alarm takes over the screen; with the app closed, the system alarm rings and "I'm up" or "Solve" opens the challenge. The eyes-open, walk and shake challenges need a device with those sensors; math and typing work everywhere. Emergency skip (bottom of the challenge screen) ends a morning without the challenge. No account is needed.
