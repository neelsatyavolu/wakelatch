# Wakelatch privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Wakelatch collects no data.

- No accounts, no sign-in.
- No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
- The app has no network code. Nothing you enter or do in Wakelatch leaves your phone.
- Your alarm times, settings and morning history are stored only on your device, and are deleted when you delete the app.

## Camera and motion

- The **eyes-open** challenge uses the front (Face ID) camera to check that your eyes are open. The camera feed is processed on the device in real time. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
- The **walk** challenge uses the phone's step counter, and the **shake** challenge uses its motion sensors. Step counts and motion readings are used only while the challenge is on screen and are not stored.

You only grant these permissions if you choose those challenges.

## Alarms

Wakelatch schedules alarms through Apple's AlarmKit so they ring even when the app is closed. Apple's system handles those alarms on your device.
